Jazeera Paints · Social Media

Ramadan 2022 Campaign

Matching the colors of familiar Ramadan drinks and foods to the Jazeera Paints palette.

The Challenge

The management team wanted a fresh, unconventional approach to Ramadan advertising, moving beyond traditional paint promotions.

The Creative Idea

Every Egyptian home shares the same Ramadan table. I built the concept around its familiar drinks and traditional foods, matching each one’s color to a shade from the Jazeera Paints palette. Everyday Ramadan moments became a clear, engaging visual campaign.

My Role

I led the creative direction and design execution, delivering a series of 7 designs where the idea carries the message and the visuals stay clean.

The Result

The campaign went beyond its original Egyptian market and was rolled out across Jazeera Paints platforms in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Iraq, under my supervision. A culturally rooted idea proved it could travel across markets without losing its identity.

Integrated Advertising & Packaging

Garnier Hair Color Campaign

The product box became the poster.

The Challenge

Garnier Hair Color needed a fresh, engaging way to stand out, beyond the conventional approach of using a model’s hair color as the main advertising visual.

The Creative Concept

I turned the product packaging into the campaign’s main visual. Instead of a model-based advertisement, the box itself became the poster, putting the promised hair color front and center.

Execution

I developed the concept into a cohesive campaign across three touchpoints: the product packaging, advertising visuals, and a custom Snapchat filter. Each one reinforced the same idea, making the brand experience clear, interactive and easy to understand.

The Result

One simple idea carried across product design, advertising and digital interaction, creating a unified and accessible brand experience.

Social Media Campaign

Hosny Restaurant

Authenticity told through the nostalgia of classic Egyptian music.

The Challenge

Hosny is an authentic, home-style restaurant, and the challenge was to make people feel that authenticity on social media, beyond simply showing dishes.

The Creative Concept

I expressed authenticity through nostalgia. The campaign linked Hosny’s food to the golden age of Egyptian music, with references to Umm Kulthum, Farid al-Atrash, Shadia, Abdel Halim Hafez and Asmahan, evoking the original taste we grew up with. To balance that classic tone with a more playful, popular voice, I added a reference to Ahmed Zaki’s song from the film Kaboria.

Execution

I designed a series of 8 social media visuals that pair strong food photography with these musical references, so each post carries both the appetite and the memory.

Key Design Decisions

  • Music instead of the usual “homemade” claim: nostalgia proves authenticity better than words.
  • Mixing eras: classic voices for the nostalgia, a lighter popular reference for a wider audience.
